About Us

OUR STORY

About us

Planet G is a landscaping and garden management company dedicated to creating sustainable, attractive, and environmentally friendly green spaces. Since our establishment in 2004, we have grown steadily and now provide landscaping services across Vapi and Mumbai.

As interest in greener spaces has grown among homeowners, businesses, and industries, Planet G has developed into a professionally managed company specializing in garden management, landscape design, and green-space development. We are committed to promoting greenery and caring for the environment, and we continue to deliver landscaping solutions that enhance the beauty and value of each property.

WHAT WE DO

Branch 01

DESIGN & BUILD

We design and deliver complete landscaping projects for corporate and residential clients, from planning through installation.

  • Turnkey residential and corporate landscaping End-to-end execution from initial concept to completed green space.
  • Low-maintenance garden design We create attractive, low-maintenance gardens suited to each property.
  • Terrace and society gardens We design and maintain terrace gardens and gardens for residential societies.
Branch 02

GARDEN CARE

We provide ongoing garden maintenance to keep outdoor spaces healthy, thriving, and well cared for.

  • Garden maintenance Consistent scheduled care to keep outdoor environments lush and healthy.
  • Trained teams and supervisors Our trained team members and supervisors support installation and ongoing care.
  • Modern mowing and pruning equipment Advanced equipment for lawn mowing, hedge trimming, and garden maintenance.
  • Clear work and maintenance records Accurate tracking and documentation of completed work and maintenance visits.
Branch 03

SPECIALTY GARDENS & FEATURES

Unique custom landscape additions, water features, and natural food-growing solutions.

  • Organic kitchen gardens We create organic kitchen gardens for growing fresh produce at home.
  • Waterfall construction We construct custom garden features, including natural rock waterfalls.
