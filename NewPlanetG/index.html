WELCOME TO PLANET G

Crafting Beautiful
Green Spaces in Mumbai

Planet G is a Landscape Design & Garden Maintenance Company Since 2004. At Planet G, we create and maintain beautiful green spaces for homes, offices, bungalows, restaurants, factories, terraces, residential societies, and public gardens. With 20+ years of experience, our team of skilled landscape professionals designs spaces that bring nature into every environment — and keeps them thriving long after. Our speciality: Expert garden and lawn maintenance. Whether it's a private flat, a housing society, or a large commercial property, our dedicated team ensures your green spaces stay lush, healthy, and picture-perfect all year round.
