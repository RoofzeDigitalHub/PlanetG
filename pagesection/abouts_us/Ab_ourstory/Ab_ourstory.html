About us

Planet G is a landscaping and garden management company dedicated to creating sustainable, attractive, and environmentally friendly green spaces. Since our establishment in 2004, we have grown steadily and now provide landscaping services across Vapi and Mumbai. As interest in greener spaces has grown among homeowners, businesses, and industries, Planet G has developed into a professionally managed company specializing in garden management, landscape design, and green-space development. We are committed to promoting greenery and caring for the environment, and we continue to deliver landscaping solutions that enhance the beauty and value of each property.
