About us

Planet G is an Landscaping and Garden Management Company dedicated to creating sustainable, aesthetically pleasing, and environmentally friendly green spaces. Since our establishment in 2006, we have grown steadily and become a trusted name for landscaping services in Vapi and surrounding areas.

As environmental awareness increased among homeowners, businesses, and industries, Planet G emerged as a professionally managed organization specializing in garden management, landscape design, horticulture, and green space development. With a strong commitment to promoting greenery and conserving the ecosystem, we continue to deliver innovative and sustainable landscaping solutions that enhance the beauty and value of every property.
